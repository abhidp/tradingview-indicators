## 🌟 Understanding the Star Rating System

The indicator scores each signal based on 5 factors:

**★ (1 star)** - Weak signal, barely met criteria
**★★ (2 stars)** - Marginal signal, basic conditions met
**★★★ (3 stars)** - Good signal, multiple factors aligned
**★★★★ (4 stars)** - Strong signal, excellent setup
**★★★★★ (5 stars)** - Premium signal, all factors at extremes

**Trading Tip:** Focus on 4-5 star signals near major support/resistance. These are your highest probability setups.

- **Minimum Signal Strength (1-5)** → Set to 2-3 for balanced signals, 4-5 for only the best

### Advanced Settings (For Experienced Traders)

**Volatility Adjustment**

- **Use ATR-Based Wick Threshold** → Market moving wild? Indicator adjusts automatically
- Prevents false signals in high volatility

**Support/Resistance Context**

- **Require S/R Context** → Only show signals near swing highs/lows
- **S/R Proximity % (1.0)** → How close to S/R level (1% default)

**Effort vs Result Analysis**

- **Enable Effort vs Result** → Classic VSA: detects when big bars produce small moves
- Shows institutional absorption (smart money quietly accumulating/distributing)

**Multi-Bar Pattern Detection**

- **Require Multi-Bar Pattern** → Looks for climactic bars followed by quiet testing
- Classic Wyckoff sequence: panic/euphoria → professional control

**Confirmation**

- **Require Next Bar Confirmation** → Wait for follow-through before signaling
- Reduces signals but increases reliability

## 📚 What is VSA? (Volume Spread Analysis)

VSA was developed by Tom Williams based on the work of Richard Wyckoff in the early 1900s. The core principle:

**"Follow the smart money."**

### The Big Players Leave Footprints

- **Large volume** = Institutions are active (accumulation/distribution)
- **Low volume** = Retail traders only, institutions sitting out
- **Wide spread on low volume** = Weakness (no demand/supply)
- **Narrow spread on high volume** = Absorption (institutions soaking up orders)

### Why Low Volume Matters

When price can't push through on **low volume**, it shows:

- At highs → Nobody wants to buy (No Demand) = Bearish
- At lows → Nobody wants to sell (No Supply) = Bullish

This indicator automates this analysis and adds intelligent filtering.
